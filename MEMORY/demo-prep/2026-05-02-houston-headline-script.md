Audience: Todd (CEO), James (COO/CRO), Ameen (CFO), Kyle (CTO).
Total time: ~15 min for the demo proper, with 2 min open + 1 min close.
Win condition: Todd, James, and Ameen leave wanting to greenlight a paid pilot.

This script replaces the original Nashville/Pittsburgh/LA arc with **Houston as the headline branch**. The pivot is honest: federal procurement data is structurally thinner in those three target metros within a 30-day window (verified after Z-F finish + Option B shipped — see `MEMORY/demo-prep/2026-05-02-zedcor-demo-cleanup.md`).
