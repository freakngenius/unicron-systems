# Building Your Pathfinder — Quick Intake

Six questions. Fifteen minutes. Answer however works for you — typed, voice memo, video, or quick call.

We use these answers to build you a small-scale tailored demo and feed our Architect to design a system that surfaces real opportunities for your business. A deeper intake comes after if you want to keep going.

---

## 1. The win in one sentence

Describe one ideal opportunity for your business — a deal, a customer, a contract, an asset — in a sentence or two. What's it worth in dollars when you close one?

---

## 2. Where you operate

What cities, regions, or markets matter most? Top 3-5 is enough.

---

## 3. The signals that tell you "now"

What events tell you a deal is becoming available, or that someone is becoming a buyer? List as many as come to mind. Examples: distressed filings, broker listings, executive moves, RFP releases, regulatory changes, news mentions, permit filings, funding rounds, layoffs.

This is the most important question. The denser your answer, the more accurate the system.

---

## 4. What makes it real

When you spot a potential opportunity, what do you check before acting? What disqualifies it immediately?

---

## 5. Where you already look

What websites, databases, publications, or sources do you check today to find or qualify these opportunities? List by name.

---

## 6. The dream

If a tailored system was already running for you tomorrow morning, what would you want to see in your inbox? What would make you say "this is worth paying for"?

---

## Optional but useful

**7.** Any regulations, compliance constraints, or things we should never say in outreach? (SEC, HIPAA, accredited investor, ESG, industry-specific.)

**8.** What word does your team use for these — leads, deals, opportunities, prospects, properties, projects? We use your language.

---

## What we do with your answers

Within 48 hours of receiving these, we send back:

- A draft architecture — sources we'd watch, signals we'd track, a scoring approach, all tailored to you.
- A small live demo of the system surfacing real opportunities matching your inputs.
- A short follow-up call (15 min) to refine.

If you want to keep going, we send a deeper intake survey covering pipeline stages, integrations, outreach style, and how your team operates. That's where the system gets dialed.
