# Building Your Pathfinder — Intake Survey

A handful of questions so we can build a system that surfaces the right opportunities for your business — not a generic template. The denser your answers, the more accurate the system. Anything you don't know, leave blank or note UNKNOWN.

Send in writing, audio note, or a 30-min call. Whatever's fastest.

---

## 1. The win

**1a.** Describe one ideal opportunity for your business in one sentence (the "lead unit").

**1b.** Dollar value of one win? (Range fine. Note recurring revenue or repeat business.)

**1c.** How many wins per year today? Goal in 12 months?

**1d.** Single biggest deal/win you've ever closed. What made it different?

---

## 2. Where you compete

**2a.** Geography — cities, regions, states, countries that matter. Top 3-5.

**2b.** Geographies you explicitly do not pursue.

**2c.** Actively expanding into new markets? Which?

---

## 3. Trigger signals — what tells you "now"

Most important section. We watch the world for these.

**3a.** What events tell you a deal is becoming available or someone is becoming a buyer? List as many as you can:
- Distressed debt filings, foreclosures, bankruptcies
- Layoffs, RFP releases, executive hires/departures
- Regulatory or policy changes
- Broker listings, new construction permits
- News mentions, press releases, M&A activity
- Trade show activity, conference appearances
- Funding rounds, IPOs, acquisitions
- Customer complaints, churn signals

**3b.** Times of year, market cycles, or external conditions that drive deal flow up/down?

**3c.** Looking back at your best deals, what was the trigger event that started each one?

---

## 4. Data you already watch

**4a.** Websites, databases, feeds, publications you check regularly. List by name.

**4b.** What you wish you could monitor but don't have time for.

**4c.** Proprietary data that could sharpen scoring (CRM, past deal performance, customer profiles). We won't access without sign-off — just want to know what exists.

**4d.** Third-party data feeds/services you pay for today (CoStar, ZoomInfo, Bloomberg, industry-specific).

---

## 5. What "verified" means

**5a.** When you see a potential opportunity, what do you check before acting? (verification criteria)

**5b.** What disqualifies an opportunity immediately? (deal-breaker filters)

**5c.** Difference between "warm" and "qualified" lead in your world?

---

## 6. How you pursue

**6a.** Walk us through your current pipeline. Stages from "first heard" to "won." Use your team's stage names.

**6b.** Usual first-touch outreach? (email, broker call, intro, referral) Tone that works in your industry?

**6c.** Who acts on these — just you or a team? How big?

**6d.** What systems do they use? (CRM, email, calendar, deal-room, project management)

**6e.** What does a successful first conversation look like?

---

## 7. Compliance & constraints

**7a.** Regulatory constraints (SEC, HIPAA, FERPA, accredited investor, ESG, GDPR, industry-specific).

**7b.** Restrictions on prospect communication? (no cold email, opt-in only, broker-only contact)

**7c.** Integrations that matter (CRM, email, calendar, deal-room, accounting, billing).

**7d.** Anything we absolutely should not say or claim in outreach.

---

## 8. The adjacent

**8a.** If we surfaced opportunities outside your core but in adjacent space, useful? Examples?

**8b.** Who else in your organization could benefit from the same intelligence? (cross-functional)

**8c.** Other companies, partners, networks worth tracking alongside direct opportunities?

---

## 9. Your language

**9a.** What word do you use for "lead"? "deal"? "opportunity"? "prospect"? "customer"?

**9b.** Internal terminology, acronyms, jargon (asset class names, deal types, internal product names, status terms).

---

## 10. What hasn't worked

**10a.** Approaches you tried that didn't stick (manual scraping, hiring SDRs, outsourced research, paid lead lists).

**10b.** What's still painful about how you do this today?

---

## 11. The dream

**11a.** If a system was already running for you tomorrow, what would the perfect first 5 minutes of looking at it look like?

**11b.** What would you want in your inbox each morning?

**11c.** What makes you say "this is worth $X/month"? What's X, and what does the system have to do to earn it?

---

## 12. About you & your team

**12a.** Your name, role, decision authority on this kind of system.

**12b.** Who else on your team should we hear from? (1-2 names)

**12c.** Timeline for deciding whether something like this is for you?

---
