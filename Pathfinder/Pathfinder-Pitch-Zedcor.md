# Pathfinder — Pitch for Zedcor

**Audience:** Kyle Doenz (CTO) and the Zedcor business partners who approve the rollout.
**Length:** 60-90 second read or spoken pitch.
**Frame:** This is not a SaaS we want to sell you. It's an agent operation we run for you, embedded in your data center, on your compute, against your data.

---

Pathfinder is an agent operation that surfaces qualified construction security opportunities 60-90 days before your competitors find out they exist, and unlocks warm-intro revenue paths between your branches that are invisible today.

Today your salespeople drive territories and discover projects when fences go up. By then, budgets are set and a security provider has been chosen. You're boxed out before the conversation starts. ZoomInfo enriches contacts but doesn't surface pre-budget construction signals. No system in the market does this for multi-branch field-sales orgs serving jobsites — until now.

**How it works, end to end:**

Three Perplexity Computer agents run continuously in your environment, on your idle L4 GPUs in the Dallas data center. They monitor every public construction signal across the US and Canada — federal contract awards, state DOT projects, county permits, press releases, project announcements — pull them in, deduplicate them, and decide what's worth your team's attention.

The **Ingestor agent** runs every 6 hours. It browses jurisdiction portals, hits federal and state APIs, reads press wires, and synthesizes records that describe the same project from different sources into a single normalized entity. Roughly 250 new project signals per week across your footprint.

The **Ranker agent** cross-references each new project against your branch geography and your existing customer base — running locally on your data so nothing private leaves your data center. For each project, it decides: is this in branch X's coverage? Is it within reach of a customer that branch Y already serves? What stage is the project in? When does the security RFP cycle typically open for this contract type? Then it writes a one-paragraph rationale and a recommended outreach hook in Zedcor's voice.

The **Adjacent agent** runs weekly to identify similar multi-branch field-sales opportunities — not for your sales motion, for our pattern-replication and growth.

**What your branch managers and reps see:**

A ranked Slack digest every Friday morning, per branch. Top 15 projects, scored, with reasoning. One tap to accept a lead and push it into HubSpot. One tap to dismiss. The system learns from rejection and sharpens.

**Two things happen as a result:**

You stop arriving late. The Port of Houston Terminal C expansion gets surfaced 9 days after the federal contract is awarded — 60-90 days before any security RFP. Your Houston team starts the relationship pre-budget.

Cross-pollination revenue: Lyondell already pays your Houston branch. Their new build in the Atlanta branch's territory is now visible. Atlanta's GM walks in with a warm-intro referral instead of a cold call.

**The economics:**

At your $2.5B target by 2030, Zedcor needs to add roughly $400M in new ARR every year. Even a 5% lift in qualified pipeline volume is $20M+ in additional ARR. Pathfinder runs on your idle compute, costs around $0.003 per ranked lead, and replaces what would otherwise be 4 FTEs you've considered hiring and never did because the math doesn't work. Two-person operating team plus AI agents producing the work of a fifty-person research org.

**The framing:**

We didn't ship you software. We embedded an agent operation inside your data center, ran it on your compute against your data, and made your sales team materially earlier and materially more leveraged.

That's the new shape of B2B AI. Zedcor is client zero.
