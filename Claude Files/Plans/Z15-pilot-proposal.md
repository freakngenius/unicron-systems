## The problem we solve
When a general contractor is named on a construction project, subcontractors and service providers have a short window, roughly two to four weeks, to get onto the GC's list before it locks. For Zedcor's mobile surveillance towers, hitting that window on the right projects is the difference between winning the job and never being in the conversation. Tracking it manually across every county, city, school district, and DOT in your markets is not feasible by hand.

## What Pathfinder does
Pathfinder continuously monitors public construction opportunity sources, identifies projects entering the post-award buy window, and delivers a prioritized lead feed directly into Notion where your reps already work. Each actionable lead arrives phase-tagged, scored, and packaged with specific pitch hooks tied to a Zedcor capability and a recommended next action with a target date. Reps get a Slack alert the moment a project enters the window.
